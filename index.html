Set2Print

Turn a brick set's parts list into ready-to-print 3MF plates, one batch per colour. Everything runs in your browser: parts come from the LDraw library and nothing is uploaded.

1 · Parts list

Every official set is built in.

One part per line: part, colour, quantity. Colour can be a name or a Rebrickable colour number. Rebrickable CSV exports work as they are.

Drop a .csv here, or

2 · Print options

Gap leaves room for a brim between parts. Real bricks clutch hardest at 100%; try 99.5% if prints are too tight.

Automatic supports: every underside is checked by geometry. Bridges up to 20 mm and overhangs up to about 70° print as they are; a part that fails is turned onto its best side, then gets breakaway pillars under what still overhangs, and fins from Support Fins. Print with slicer supports off.

Example: enter 6000-1 with your key, or paste a list, then Build plates.

Output: one 3MF per plate, named by colour, plus each unique part as an STL. Open the plates in Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer. Parts print studs-up; turn slicer supports off and add fins where needed with Support Fins.

Part geometry: LDraw.org parts library (CC BY 2.0, by its many authors). Set inventories and colours: Rebrickable. Loader: three.js.
